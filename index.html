About

Data Analyst & QA Tester · Newcastle, UK · Open to work

I'm originally from Myanmar (Burma) and now based in Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom. I like working with data — cleaning it, querying it and turning it into something people can actually use to make a decision — and I like finding what's wrong with it, whether that's an error in a dataset or a bug in an app. Either way, I check before I trust a result. SQL, Excel and Power BI are my main tools, I've used Python, Apache Spark and Azure on larger projects, and I'm studying for the ISTQB Foundation Level certificate in software testing.

I graduated from Northumbria University in July 2026 and I'm now applying for data analyst and QA roles while building real projects to back it up. I'm open to relocating within the UK or Southeast Asia for the right role.
